Columns: pitch, track instrument, velocity, length in ticks (480 = quarter note).
=== means a held note; OFF marks its ending row. * marks an off-grid start.
Exact timing and overlapping notes are preserved; chords use extra columns.
e opens: pitch velocity length start. Example: C#4 100 360 17 (or 61 for C#4).
Mouse: click selects a cell; right click deletes; wheel scrolls time;
Shift+wheel scrolls columns. Switching views never converts project data.

Any key closes help. All track, playback, recording, and file commands are shared.
